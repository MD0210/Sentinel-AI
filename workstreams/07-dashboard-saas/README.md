# 07 — Dashboard and SaaS

## Purpose

Evolve Sentinel from a local development tool into a product that users or customer teams can operate and manage.

## Scope

- Agent, task, and execution-status dashboard.
- Hierarchical-agent visibility and controls.
- Pluggable authentication and workspace boundaries.
- Usage metering, quotas, and pricing experiments.
- Deployment, monitoring, backup, and support processes.
- Customer discovery and first-customer pilot.
